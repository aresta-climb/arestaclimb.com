**3. Evidence Collection, Camera, and Geolocation (GPS):** To ensure traceability and transparency for the safety of the climbing community, Fixalog collects high-precision geographic coordinates (GPS) and detailed photographic records of each inspected anchor point. This data is embedded and digitally signed in the issued technical reports.

**4. Cryptographic Integrity and Open Licensing:** Generated technical reports contain digital signatures, cryptographic digests (SHA-256 hashes), and tamper-evident metadata.

# Privacy Policy - Fixalog

The **Fixalog** application was developed by the Aresta Climb ecosystem as a technical tool focused on safety, documentation, and auditing of fixed anchors on climbing routes.

This policy clearly and transparently describes which permissions are used, how your data is stored locally, and how your information is protected.

---

## 1. Local Storage Principle and Data Sovereignty (Offline-First)

Fixalog is built with a strictly **offline-first** architecture:
- Your identity data (name, public email, public key, and maintainer certificate) and all reports in progress are stored **exclusively in the local database (SQLite)** on your device.
- No photos, videos, or geographic coordinates of audited routes are automatically transmitted to remote servers without your explicit share or export command.

---

## 2. Requested Device Permissions

To enable technical route auditing, Fixalog requests the following operating system permissions:

### A. Camera
- **Purpose:** Capturing detailed photos and videos of fixed anchors (bolts, hangers, belay stations, steel alloy, and corrosion wear).
- **Use:** Media files are stored in the local session folder and embedded into the final signed EPUB 3 and PDF forensic reports. The camera is never accessed in the background.

### B. Precise Location (GPS)
- **Purpose:** Recording geographic coordinates (latitude, longitude, and approximate altitude) at the time of inspection.
- **Use:** Ensures that the technical report proves the exact location of the intervention on the crag, preventing misidentification of routes.

---

## 3. Cryptography and Digital Signature

Technical reports generated by Fixalog use the maintainer's local cryptographic keys to digitally sign reports (X.509 XML-DSig). The private signing key remains protected in your device's secure enclave (Secure Storage / Keystore) and is never sent to any external server.

---

## 4. Diagnostic Data and Crash Monitoring (Crashlytics and Sentry)

To ensure continuous application stability, Fixalog collects anonymous technical crash reports and unhandled exceptions via **Firebase Crashlytics** and **Sentry**. No anchor photos, inspection GPS coordinates, or private cryptographic keys are included in error reports.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR / LGPD)

Because your data remains under your direct control on your own device, you can at any time modify or delete your identity data on the Settings screen, delete your local report history, and revoke Camera and Location permissions in your operating system settings.
